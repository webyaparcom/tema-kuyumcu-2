Our workshop

From founding to today

From one bench to a showcase

Move the slider through the years.

1 / 5

  1. 1979

    A first bench in the bazaar

    We opened as a one-bench workshop in the Bakircilar Bazaar.

  2. 1994

    Our own casting furnace

    We began casting wedding bands and twist rings in our own moulds.

  3. 2008

    The showcase store

    A store where customers choose pieces after weighing them was added beside the workshop.

  4. 2019

    Gem-set collection

    We designed solitaire and trilogy lines with certified stones.

  5. 2026

    Private viewings

    Private viewings by appointment began for engagement and wedding jewellery.

The same six seals on every piece
